YouTube Creator Aaron Denvil Reported Missing After Cryptic Tweet

by Leah Monroe · Published: July 18, 2018 · Category: Internet / Community
IMAGE
NOT AVAILABLE
File Photo: A promotional still from one of Denvil’s horror livestream thumbnails. The original asset could not be loaded from the Poly Articles media archive.

Independent YouTube creator Aaron “Denvil” Harrow, known online for his series of long-form breakdowns of obscure indie horror games, has been reported missing according to local authorities in his hometown of Red Ridge.

According to police, Denvil was last seen on the evening of July 12, 2018. Friends say he left his apartment briefly and did not return. His disappearance has sparked concern among his online audience of roughly 118,000 subscribers, many of whom have been sharing screenshots, clips, and theories across social media.

“He kept joking about ‘testing something with the middlemen’ on stream,” one regular viewer, who asked not to be named, told Poly Articles. “We thought it was some new ARG or series he was planning.”

The last confirmed activity from any of Denvil’s official accounts is a short tweet posted at 10:42 PM the same night he disappeared. The tweet, which has since been widely circulated, simply reads:

"middlemen don't sleep"

No follow-up posts were made. Shortly after, Denvil’s channel banner and profile picture were changed to a plain black background. Comment sections on his recent videos are now flooded with viewers asking if the tweet was part of a scripted bit or a cryptic sign that something had gone wrong.

One of Denvil’s long-time collaborators, who streams under the name “chroma_fall”, told Poly Articles that the creator had been working on what he described as a “bigger than usual” project in the weeks leading up to his disappearance, involving “old feeds, archived accounts, and a lot of late-night testing.” They declined to share more details on the record.

“He was exhausted,” chroma_fall said. “He kept saying stuff like ‘If this works, nobody will have to play alone again’ and ‘I finally figured out how to make the middleman talk back.’ I assumed it was about some AI thing or some narrative project.”

Law enforcement has not confirmed whether they consider Denvil’s online activity relevant to his disappearance, but did acknowledge that they are aware of the final tweet and other posts circulating in fan communities.

A pinned message on Denvil’s channel from early June references an upcoming series described as a “deep dive into the gaps between platforms, the spaces where no one looks.” It does not provide a title or release date.

Police are asking anyone with information about Denvil’s whereabouts to contact the Red Ridge tip line. In the meantime, fans continue to comb through old streams and VODs for clues, with some insisting that the answer to his last message might already be hidden somewhere in his backlog.

Poly Articles will update this story if more information becomes available.
(Editor’s note: As of July 24, 2018, no further official statements have been released.)
